## Limits

- HIST option prices are MODELED: one flat IV per day, no smile, no intraday IV change, a price every minute, an expiry every session (before Nov 2022 SPY/QQQ/IWM did not list one every weekday). Next-expiry HIST tapes expire at the next NYSE session and use run 4's 0DTE multiplier where run 4 has no next-expiry key (labeled fallback): NOT VALIDATED; DEV REAL decides.
- The D option twins, A-opt and the A3 twins run in tools/sprint_round8.py's option pass (one pass per entry), with the engine's own fill rules; they are not rows in the engine's run log (their trials are counted in the 192).
- Signal-type option exits flip (sell the held contract, buy the other side at the next print after the same decision); grid cells hold one position at a time and skip signals while open.
- The matched-random option benchmark is one random-side path (seed in the spec) per rule and contract, with the same 13 exits.
- A3 is source grade C (a ~2-year blog test): a pass is exploratory. D5/D6 are designed tests of Nick's idea with no source: D5 must also beat its made-up levels.
- 2% half-spread may be optimistic for thinner 2016-2019 0DTE markets; share costs assume $0.01 a share each way.
