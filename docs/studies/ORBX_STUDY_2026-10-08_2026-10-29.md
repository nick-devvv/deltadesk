## How to read this

An interval that includes 0 means the sample can't tell the strategy from zero (or from its random controls). Small n per exit is expected: the sequence (break, independent candle, retest) is rare by design. None of this qualifies ORBX for anything; it starts as a separate paper-research ledger and needs forward, later-dated evidence that beats matched controls after costs.
